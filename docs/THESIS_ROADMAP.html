CHAPTER 1 · The ProblemPendahuluan — Why This Research Exists

NARRATIVE HOOK FOR DEFENSE

Opening: "Indonesia has 127,000 koperasi managing assets over ~$10 billion — yet 40% are inactive. Four major fraud cases — Indosurya (Rp106T), Pandawa (Rp3.9T), Lima Garuda, Intidana — prove that traditional governance fails. Blockchain offers transparency, but existing DAO models use token-weighted voting which conflicts with Pasal 22 UU 25/1992 (1 member = 1 vote). No one has ever mapped Indonesia's cooperative law into executable smart contracts. This is the gap we fill."
